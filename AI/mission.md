4. AI Engineering Lens: What You Will Actually Automate As An Intern

Forget building a new ChatGPT. Real internship tasks look like this:

Task 1 — Inbox Triage Automation: Company gets 200 emails a day. Your job: AI reads email, classifies it — Complaint, Order, Spam, Urgent — extracts customer name + order ID, routes to right person. Human still replies to Urgent. You save 2 hours a day.

Task 2 — WhatsApp Order Bot: Customer sends "I want 20 bags." AI checks stock via tool, checks payment via tool, books delivery, sends WhatsApp confirmation. If anything is unclear, it hands over to human. No hallucinated prices because it uses RAG.

Task 3 — Document Summarizer: Manager gets 50-page PDF. AI extracts: What is the decision? Who owns what? By when? You use RAG + structured output, not free chat.

Task 4 — Internal Knowledge Assistant: Staff asks "What is our refund policy?" AI searches company docs, answers with source: "From Policy Page 3..." If not in docs, it says "I don't know" — that's Fail Closed for AI.
